# RozgaarAI Demo Guide

## Judge Story

RozgaarAI turns informal worker experience into a trusted employment identity, then connects workers, employers, and NGOs through consent-first hiring infrastructure.

## Recommended Walkthrough

1. Start on the landing page and explain the challenge: skilled workers remain invisible without trusted digital proof.
2. Open Worker Demo and show a complete worker identity, income passport, resume, job matching, interview coach, and safety assistant.
3. Open Employer Demo and show verified worker discovery, applicants, pipeline, messages, analytics, and company settings.
4. Open NGO Demo and show consent-first worker support, training, job opportunities, placement pipeline, reports, team management, and audit logs.
5. Open `/admin/diagnostics` to show production readiness checks.

## Demo Safety

- Demo workspaces use sample overlays and session state.
- Exit Demo returns to clean real workspace states.
- Real workspaces do not show fake applicants, fake jobs, fake analytics, or fake messages.

## Pitch Assets

- Problem: informal skill exists, trusted proof does not.
- Solution: voice-first identity, income passport, AI matching, and consent-led NGO support.
- Differentiator: three-sided workflow for workers, employers, and NGOs.
- Pilot path: NGO cohort, employer partners, consented recommendations, measured outcomes.
