# RozgaarAI Real-World Pilot Guide

## Pilot Goals

- Validate whether workers can create trusted digital career identities with NGO assistance.
- Validate employer confidence in verified informal worker profiles.
- Measure consent completion, profile completion, interview movement, and placements.

## Recommended Pilot Cohort

- 1 NGO or foundation partner.
- 20 to 50 workers across domestic work, electrical work, plumbing, tailoring, driving, and care work.
- 3 to 5 employer partners with verified hiring needs.

## Operating Model

1. NGO admin creates or completes the organization profile.
2. NGO staff invite or assist workers only with consent.
3. Workers complete identity, skills, availability, and wage expectations.
4. NGO reviews training readiness and certificate status.
5. Employers publish real opportunities.
6. NGO recommends workers with consent.
7. Interview, selection, joining, and follow-up records are tracked.

## Pilot Metrics

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Worker profile completion | 80%+ |
| Consent granted | 90%+ of linked workers |
| Employer response time | Under 48 hours |
| Interview conversion | 30%+ of recommended workers |
| Placement follow-up completion | 80%+ |

## Data Rules

- Workers own their identity.
- NGOs assist, verify, and recommend only with permission.
- Demo workers are never mixed into real pilot reporting.
- Sensitive documents should use production storage policies before live upload.

## Weekly Review

- Review audit log.
- Export placement and consent reports.
- Check stuck recommendations and follow-ups.
- Capture qualitative feedback from workers, NGO operators, and employers.
